How it works

Set up once.
Use every day.

01
We onboard your agency
We configure your private dashboard instance with your agency name, branding, agent roster, and default market. Takes less than 24 hours.
02
Your agents log in
Every agent gets their own secure login. Data is scoped to your agency — fully isolated from every other brokerage on the platform.
03
Fill in the details
Each AI agent has a simple intake form — property address, homeowner name, MLS data, tone. No prompting knowledge required.
04
Generate & export
Claude builds the output instantly. Export directly to Follow Up Boss, download as CSV or JSON, or copy to clipboard — ready in seconds.
